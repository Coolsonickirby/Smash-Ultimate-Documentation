# Unlock Final Smash, Special Hit, and Assist Camera

Normally, when you use a Final Smash, land a special hit (ex. Deadly Hit), or have an assist on the field, the Camera option is disabled to prevent issues that can occur. This feature removes that restriction and allows you to use it (though you can encounter issues.)

![type:video](<../img/The CSK Collection/unlock_camera.mp4#center>)

<div align="center"><sup>Used alongside the Unrestircted Camera mod</sup></div>


To enable this feature, create a file called `mods:/flags/unlock_camera.flag`.

It's recommended that when using this feature, you use the [Unrestircted Camera mod](https://gamebanana.com/mods/38659) created by [ThatNintendoNerd/TNN](https://github.com/ThatNintendoNerd), as some Final Smashes happen where the controllable camera can't move to.
