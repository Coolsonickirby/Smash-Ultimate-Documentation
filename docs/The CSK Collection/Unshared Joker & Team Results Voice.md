# Unshared Joker & Team Results Voice

By default, Joker's result voice (the one where his teammates and himself speak after winning) have an issue where if you try to unshare his victory result voice, it won't work and just be silence instead.

To use this feature, you can either grab the "Joker One-Slot Voice Config" mod that comes with the CSK Collection or create a new mod folder with the following `config.json` below, then rename your modded `vc_jack_result_cXX.nus3audio` files to `vc_jack_result_cXX_mod.nus3audio`.
